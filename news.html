Recent developments

Recent papers, talks and course highlights.

  • Spring 2025

    Stars of CSE 564 Spring 2025: playlist of the best projects from this year’s graduate visualization course.

  • IEEE VIS 2025

    Five accepted papers at IEEE VIS 2025.

    • MisVisFix: An Interactive Dashboard for Detecting, Explaining, and Correcting Misleading Visualizations using Large Language Models with Amit Kumar Das
    • What is the Color of Serendipity? Investigating the Use of Language Models for Semantically Resonant Color Generation with Shahreen Salim and Tanzir Pial
    • Charts-of-Thought: Enhancing LLM Visualization Literacy Through Structured Data Extraction with Amit Kumar Das and Mohammad Tarun
    • Semantic Pathway: An Interactive Visualization of Hidden States and Token Influence in LLMs with Mithilesh Singh
    • XplainAct: Visualization for Personalized Intervention Insights with Yanming Zhang and Krishnakumar Hegde
  • IEEE TVCG

    Two recently accepted papers in IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics (TVCG).

    • CausalChat: Interactive Causal Model Development and Refinement Using Large Language Models with Yanming Zhang, Akshith Kotha, and Eric Papenhausen
    • Into the Void: Mapping the Unseen Gaps in High Dimensional Data with Xinyu Zhang, Tyler Estro, Geoff Kuenning, and Erez Zadok
  • CHI / CSCW

    Three recent papers in human-centered responsible AI: fairness, belief modeling, and personalized communication.

    • ACM CHI 2025: Leveraging Large Language Models for Personalized Public Messaging with Amit Kumar Das and Cindy Xiong Bearfield
    • ACM CSCW 2025: FairPlay: A Collaborative Approach to Mitigate Bias in Datasets for Improved AI Fairness with Tina Behzad, Mithilesh Singh, and Anthony Ripa
    • ACM CSCW 2024: Belief Miner: A Methodology for Discovering Causal Beliefs and Causal Illusions from General Populations with Shahreen Salim and Naimul Hoque
