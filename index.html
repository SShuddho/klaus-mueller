I am a Professor in the Computer Science Department at Stony Brook University and a Senior Scientist at Brookhaven National Laboratory. I received my PhD in Computer Science from The Ohio State University in 1998. My research interests include visual analytics, explainable and responsible AI, machine learning, data science, fairness, human-centered computing, VR/AR, and computational and medical imaging.
